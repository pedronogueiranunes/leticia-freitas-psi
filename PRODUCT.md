# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People looking for a psychologist in Vitória da Conquista/BA (in-person) or online, including Brazilians living in Europe. Most arrive on mobile through Google Ads.

## Product Purpose

Site for psychologist Letícia. Success is a WhatsApp contact that becomes a first session, and then continuity from session 1 to 3.

## Positioning

Therapy grounded in behavior analysis (AC), ACT and DBT. In-person at Espaço Viver (R. Dom Climério de Andrade, 145, Recreio) and online.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS, PT/EN i18n in `js/translations.js`. Deploy: push to `main` publishes on Hostinger.
- CSS/JS are cached for 7 days: every CSS/JS change bumps `?v=` in `index.html`.
- WhatsApp buttons carry a pre-filled message per section and fire the `whatsapp_click` event with `origem`.

## Brand Commitments

- CFP 06/2019: no testimonials, no promised results, no TCC mentions.
- Copy approved by Letícia does not change without her sign-off.
- Writing: no em dashes, no typical AI-text tells.

## Evidence on Hand

Real photos of the space in `images/espaco/`, card images in `images/cards/`. No testimonials may be used.

## Product Principles

- Convert contact into a first session; reduce friction to WhatsApp.
- Calm and trustworthy, never salesy.
- Ethics rules outrank persuasion.
